# 🌙 The Hitchhiker’s Guide to the Moonz

### A Developer’s Guide to Building on Moonz

Welcome, traveller.

You have found the developer’s guide to Moonz.

Somewhere beneath the surface of moonz.fun lives a Solana program managing tokens, markets, liquidity and an entire on chain lifecycle.

This guide is your map.

Whether you are building a trading bot, token scanner, analytics platform, explorer, market dashboard or something nobody has thought of yet, Moonz gives you the tools to understand and interact with the protocol directly from Solana.

No secret infrastructure.

No private Moonz database.

Just public developer tools, on chain state and whatever you decide to build with them.

{% hint style="info" %}
**The first rule of travelling through the Moonz**

You do not need access to Moonz private infrastructure to understand Moonz.

The SDKs, IDL, program state and developer tooling in this guide are designed to let you work with the protocol directly from Solana.
{% endhint %}

## 🧭 Don’t Panic

You do not need to understand the entire Moonz protocol before you can start building.

We are going to begin with the simple stuff.

Recognise a Moonz token.

Read its state.

Follow its journey.

Understand its market.

Listen to what happens on chain.

Then, when you are ready, interact with it.

Every section of this guide will explain what you are looking at, show you how it works and give you a small example of something you can build with it.

You are not expected to memorise Moonz.

You are here to explore it.
